There are three enclosure designs, tracking two board revisions and two mounting styles. All three clip onto a standard collar strap and have a separate window over the status LED.

| Version | Fits board | Battery | Mounting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **V1R1** | V1 | 18650 Li-ion, 3500 mAh | Detachable — clips onto a separate collar piece via a plug-latch mechanism |
| **V1R2** | V1 | 18650 Li-ion, 3500 mAh | Fixed — mounts directly on the collar, not easily detachable |
| **V2R1** | V2 | 50×37×4 mm pouch cell, 1000 mAh | Fixed — mounts directly on the collar, same style as V1R2 |

The V1/V2 **board** split is about the battery gauge/IMU I2C rework (see [Battery](/LIA/docs/firmware/battery/) and [Accelerometer](/LIA/docs/firmware/accelerometer/)), not the enclosure — the enclosure split follows the battery each board is paired with. The bigger 18650 cell needs more internal volume, which is what V1R1/V1R2 are sized for; V2R1 is built around the smaller, thinner pouch cell instead, trading capacity for a noticeably more compact shell. V1R2 and V2R1 share the fixed, non-detachable mounting style — V1R1 is the only one with the separate, detachable collar piece.
