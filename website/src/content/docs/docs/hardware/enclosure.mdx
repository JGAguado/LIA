There are three enclosure designs, tracking two board revisions and two mounting styles. All three clip onto a standard collar strap and have a separate window over the status LED.

:::note
**V1.0** and **V1.1** are identical at the firmware/electronics level. The only difference between them is the battery they're built around (see [Battery](/LIA/docs/firmware/battery/)), which is what actually drives the enclosure split below.
:::

| Version | Fits board | Battery | Mounting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **V1.0-Detachable** | V1.0 | 18650 Li-ion, 3500 mAh | Detachable — clips onto a separate collar piece via a plug-latch mechanism |
| **V1.0-Fixed** | V1.0 | 18650 Li-ion, 3500 mAh | Fixed — mounts directly on the collar, not easily detachable |
| **V1.1-Fixed** | V1.1 | 50×37×4 mm pouch cell, 1000 mAh | Fixed — mounts directly on the collar, same style as V1.0-Fixed |

The bigger 18650 cell needs more internal volume, which is what the V1.0 designs are sized for; V1.1-Fixed is built around the smaller, thinner pouch cell instead, trading capacity for a noticeably more compact shell. V1.0-Fixed and V1.1-Fixed share the fixed, non-detachable mounting style — V1.0-Detachable is the only one with the separate, detachable collar piece.

Compatible with the V1.1 board and its smaller 50×37×4 mm / 1000 mAh pouch cell, which allows a noticeably more compact shell than the 18650-based V1.0 designs — the tradeoff is less capacity, so shorter runtime between charges (see [Power Management](/LIA/docs/firmware/power-management/)). Mounts directly on the collar, same fixed style as V1.0-Fixed.
