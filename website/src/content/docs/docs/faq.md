## Does LIA need a subscription?

No. LIA has no cloud backend and no recurring fee. Location data travels over the Meshtastic mesh, which is community-operated and free to use.

## Does LIA work anywhere?

Only where there's LoRa mesh coverage — either your own nodes or nearby community nodes on the same region/channel. In areas with no Meshtastic nodes at all, LIA can still log GPS fixes locally, but it can't relay them anywhere.

## Is LIA waterproof?

The enclosure is still under design, with the aim to have with a gasket seal on future revisions. but it hasn't been submersion- or pressure-tested yet — see [Enclosure](/LIA/docs/hardware/enclosure/) and [Assembly](/LIA/docs/hardware/assembly/).

## Can I build my own?

Yes — that's the point. All hardware, firmware, and CAD files are published in this repository under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](/LIA/docs/license/). See [Downloads](/LIA/docs/downloads/) for the design files and [Getting Started](/LIA/docs/getting-started/) for firmware setup.

## Can I sell LIA devices I build?

Not without permission — the license is non-commercial. See [License](/LIA/docs/license/).

## How do I get help?

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/JGAguado/LIA/issues), or check [Troubleshooting](/LIA/docs/troubleshooting/) first.
